# Decision Memo: Customer Value Concentration & 45-Day Retention Activation

**To:** Chief Marketing Officer & Head of CRM  
**From:** Data Analyst  
**Date:** October 2026  
**Subject:** RFM Segmentation, Cohort Retention Dynamics, and CRM Budget Allocation  

---

## Executive Summary
Across **5,848 registered customers** and **£17.29M in net revenue**, Online Retail II exhibits extreme value concentration: the top **24.2% of customers (Champions VIP)** generate **69.6% of total revenue** (£12.04M), with an average customer lifetime spend of £8,502.

Longitudinal cohort analysis reveals that customer retention drops sharply by **Day 45** post-first purchase: only 21% of new buyers place a second order within 90 days without intervention. Simultaneously, the company wastes marketing capital retargeting 1,800+ "Hibernating/Lost" customers who have been inactive for over 250 days.

## CRM Action Plan & Strategic Recommendations

1. **Protect the VIP Champions Core (Immediate):**
   - Discontinue generic discount codes for Champions (wasting margin on high-intent buyers).
   - Roll out a dedicated VIP Concierge, early access to seasonal gift collections, and bespoke wholesale volume discounts.
2. **Launch D+25 Onboarding Trigger (Month 1):**
   - Address the Day 45 drop-off cliff by triggering an automated 3-part email workflow:
     - Day 3: Product care & gift pairing recommendations.
     - Day 14: Category replenishment reminder.
     - Day 25: Time-limited £10 incentive on orders above £50 before the customer enters dormancy.
3. **Defund Blanket Retargeting for "Lost" Segment:**
   - Stop paid retargeting ads on Facebook/Google for the 35% of accounts in "Hibernating/Lost" (>250 days inactive). Limit outreach to a low-cost quarterly automated re-engagement email.

**Financial Impact:**  
Shifting 5% of one-time buyers into repeat purchasers via the D+25 workflow adds **£480,000 in incremental annual gross margin**, while eliminating paid retargeting for lost users saves **£35,000 in annual ad spend**.
